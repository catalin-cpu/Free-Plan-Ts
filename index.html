Free tier — deliberate restrictions & revenue levers
What Free withholds — and why
PMS sync
Manual-only forces engagement. Any host who grows past hobby use needs Starter automation.
Email
Limited to 8 scheduled emails/mo. Full automation and PMS-triggered sends require Starter.
SMS
No SMS on Free. Starter gets an 8% of MRR SMS budget (shared across Scheduled SMS and Two-way messaging). Recommended and Premium get 12%.
Branding
TS badge always on. Professional hosts who care about guest experience upgrade to remove it.
50 views/guidebook cap
Soft limit on guidebook views per guide. At 100% usage an upgrade email fires; at 130% access pauses. Multi-property hosts hit this fast.
15% commission
Online sales only. ~5 orders/mo at avg $20 = cost of Starter. Commission drops to 6% on upgrade.
Revenue levers on Free
Store 5%
$20 avg order × 5% = $1.00/order. ~12 orders/mo covers Starter cost ($12.50). Commission drops to 3% on Starter as sales grow.

Migration map (Free/Starter/Pro): upper Growth + Pro → Pro · lower Growth + upper Starter → Starter · lower Starter → Free. Churn-to-Free: a share of monthly churners (~158 hosts) park on Free instead of leaving — no MRR loss vs baseline, pure winback + referral fuel. Breakeven check: at 27% of G4s migrating to Free, paid new-G4 ARPU must reach $25.1 (+37%) to fully offset the lost cohort — the +20–30% re-tier uplift alone does not close the gap; upgrades, marketing lift and referrals must cover the rest.

Assumption rationale & benchmarks. ① Upgrade pressure (10% cohort conversion, 70% in months 1–2): the Free caps bind fast for any real host — 50 views/guidebook is consumed by ~4 bookings × 10–15 guest opens within the first 1–2 months of stays; 8 emails/mo ≈ 4 bookings × 2 scheduled messages; manual-only PMS sync breaks at the first busy turnover cycle. So pressure peaks at months 1–2, exactly the deliberate design of the Free tier. Magnitude: freemium free→paid medians run 2–5% (OpenView/ProfitWell), usage-capped products 8–15%, and this Free base skews high-intent (trial-expired fallbacks + active hosts), so 10% base / 5% pess / 15% opt. ② Marketing lift (+120% signups, 4-month ramp): removing the paywall/trial gate typically converts 2–3× more visitors to signups (visitor→signup moves from ~2% to 4–6%; TS today: 612 G1s on 29.3k web users = 2.1%). The lift is mostly conversion-side and immediate; "free guidebook" SEO/listicle traffic compounds slower — hence the ramp. +120% ≈ 1,350 signups/mo at max vs ~612 today. ③ Referral accumulation (0.045% exposure→signup): exposures = all hosts × 55% active × ~10 guest opens/mo ≈ 60–70k/mo growing with the Free base. Chain: incentivized footer banner CTR 1–3% × clicker→host-signup 2–5% (few guests are hosts) ≈ 0.03–0.09%. Benchmark: referral programs drive 5–15% of SaaS signups (Dropbox's 35% is the outlier) — defaults land at ~30 signups/mo ≈ 4–5% of G2s, growing. ⚠ Pricing red flag: at flat list prices ($12.50/$18) the paid new-G4 ARPU would be ~$11–13 vs today's $18.3 — a decrease. The +25% default assumes per-property pricing and/or add-on monetization (SMS budgets, store commission). Breakeven is +37% ($25.1).
