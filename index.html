Free tier — deliberate restrictions & revenue levers
What Free withholds — and why
PMS sync
Manual-only forces engagement. Any host who grows past hobby use needs Starter automation.
Email
Limited to 8 scheduled emails/mo. Full automation and PMS-triggered sends require Starter.
SMS
No SMS on Free. Starter gets an 8% of MRR SMS budget (shared across Scheduled SMS and Two-way messaging). Recommended and Premium get 12%.
Branding
TS badge always on. Professional hosts who care about guest experience upgrade to remove it.
50 views/guidebook cap
Soft limit on guidebook views per guide. At 100% usage an upgrade email fires; at 130% access pauses. Multi-property hosts hit this fast.
15% commission
Online sales only. ~5 orders/mo at avg $20 = cost of Starter. Commission drops to 6% on upgrade.
Revenue levers on Free
Store 5%
$20 avg order × 5% = $1.00/order. ~12 orders/mo covers Starter cost ($12.50). Commission drops to 3% on Starter as sales grow.

2 · Free → Paid motion — the upgrade engine
Of every 100 accounts entering Free in a month, X% upgrade during their lifetime:
  • 70% upgrade within months 1–2 — the Free limits (50 views/guidebook, 8 emails/mo, 1 seat, manual PMS) are hit at the first real bookings
  • The rest trickle in by month 6, reduced by Free-base churn along the way
  • Converts mirror today's paid mix: 46% choose Pro (= current Growth+Pro share of G4s), the rest Starter
  • Benchmarks: freemium medians 2–5% · usage-capped products 8–15% — default sits low because the Free pool now includes today's non-converting signups

3 · Marketing uplift — the new funnel
Marketing pushes the Free plan → more traffic, and the Free door reshapes how visitors convert:
  • Traffic uptake — paid campaigns + "free guidebook" SEO; ramps up over months
  • Visitor → G1 jumps well above today's 2.1% — the $0 no-credit-card door captures people who would never start a trial
  • G1 → Paid falls below today's 27.4% — some would-be trialists (the lower-Starter cohort) choose Free instead; the bet is that volume offsets the rate drop and direct paid G4s end up flat-to-up
  • Every G1 that doesn't go paid lands on Free (today those signups simply vanish) and feeds the §2 engine

4 · Referrals — guests & hosts inviting hosts (churn → Free feeds it)
One flywheel, two funnels feeding it: more Free accounts → more guests see the banner → more referred hosts → more traffic.
Primary — guest referrals
  • Free hosts carry the banner — it's imposed on their guidebooks. Paid hosts can activate the referral program (banner off by default, opt-in with incentive)
  • Exposures = [Free hosts + paid hosts × low effectiveness factor] × avg guest guidebook opens/mo (~8 ≈ 4 bookings × 2 guests; replace with real guide traffic)
  • Chain: incentivized banner CTR 1–3% × clicker→host-signup 2–5% ≈ 0.03–0.09% per exposure
  • Benchmark: referral programs drive 5–15% of SaaS signups (Dropbox's 35% is the outlier)
Secondary funnel — churn → Free
  • A share of the ~158 monthly churners park on Free instead of leaving — zero cost, they churned in both scenarios
  • Their guests keep seeding banner exposures from day one
  • A few come back — a small lifetime rate spread over months 6–12 (next season, new property)
