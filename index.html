Upgrade trigger — what pushes each tier up
Free → Starter
  • Adds a 2nd property → 1-GB cap hit
  • Wants to remove TS badge or brand guide
  • Needs unlimited scheduling — Free capped at 4/mo email + SMS
  • PMS sync breaks down when done manually
  • Store commission too high as sales grow
  • Co-host needs hub access (1-seat limit)
→
Starter → Recommended
  • Needs SMS or two-way messaging at scale
  • Needs more than 8 chatbot resolutions/property/month
  • Building a guest database — needs CRM
  • Professionalising brand — custom domain
  • Growing team — needs unlimited seats
  • Google Reviews automation strategy
→
Recommended → Premium
  • Passes 50 properties — volume deal needed
  • Needs custom contract or SLA terms
  • Requires dedicated account manager
  • Reseller or white-label arrangement
  • Custom feature selection or build request
Free tier — deliberate restrictions & revenue levers
What Free withholds — and why
PMS sync
Manual-only forces engagement. Any host who grows past hobby use needs Starter automation.
Email / SMS
Limited to 4 scheduled/mo. Full automation, PMS-triggered sends, and SMS campaigns require Starter.
Branding
TS badge always on. Professional hosts who care about guest experience upgrade to remove it.
1 GB cap
Second property is a hard wall. Any growth beyond a single listing forces Starter.
15% commission
Online sales only. ~5 orders/mo at avg $20 = cost of Starter. Commission drops to 6% on upgrade.
Revenue levers on Free
Store 15%
$20 avg order × 15% = $3.00/order. ~5 orders/mo covers Starter cost ($12.50). Commission drops to 6% on Starter as sales grow.
