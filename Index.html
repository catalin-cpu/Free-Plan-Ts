Chatbot tier gap: Free gets 3 chatbot interactions/month — Starter gets none. A Free user who wants more chatbot skips Starter entirely and goes straight to Premium. Starter's upgrade path rests on: branding, PMS sync, email automation, multiple GBs, 2nd seat, and the Store commission delta. Consider giving Starter a chatbot allowance (e.g. 20/month) to close this gap.
Free tier — deliberate restrictions & revenue levers
What Free withholds — and why
PMS sync
Manual-only forces engagement. Any host who grows past hobby use needs Starter automation.
Email / SMS
No proactive guest comms. Hosts who want automated pre-arrival / departure messaging must upgrade.
Branding
TS badge always on. Professional hosts who care about guest experience upgrade to remove it.
1 GB cap
Second property is a hard wall. Any growth beyond a single listing forces Starter.
20% commission
Online sales only. 1–2 upsells/month = cost of Starter. Self-converting once Store is active.
Legacy UI
New guidebook UI reserved for paid tiers. Hosts see the difference every time a guest opens a guide.
Revenue levers on Free
Store 20%
$20 avg order × 3–4 orders/mo × 20% = $12–16/mo per active Store host. 70–93 active hosts covers the $1,118/mo new MRR loss.
